## 7. Decisions

### D001 — Decision title

**Decision:** The selected approach.

**Reason:** Why it was selected.

**Evidence/Result:** User requirement, code evidence, test, benchmark, or experiment.

**Implication:** What future work should preserve or reconsider.

**Status:** Active / Superseded by D00X

## 8. Experiments / Results

### E001 — Experiment title

**Purpose:** What the experiment tested.

**Setup:** Only the configuration needed to interpret or reproduce it.

**Result:** Key measurements or observations; link large raw logs by path.

**Conclusion:** What the result supports or rules out.

## 9. Failed Attempts / Things Not To Repeat

### F001 — Attempt title

**Tried:** What was attempted.

**Result:** What happened.

**Why it failed:** Confirmed cause or clearly labelled assessment.

**Retry only if:** Conditions that would make another attempt worthwhile.

## 10. Bugs / Open Problems

### P001 — Problem title

**Symptoms:** Observable behavior.

**Known facts:** Verified evidence.

**Ruled out:** Causes excluded and the evidence for excluding them.

**Hypotheses:** Unverified explanations.

**Next investigation:** The smallest useful check.
